What the desk actually does

01

Payments walk in

Press Play BankSim. The desk replays the public BankSim file: card payments from a Spanish-bank simulator, all in Madrid, with the file’s own fraud flag.

02

Rules argue in public

Too many taps, a city jump that a plane could not make, an amount way above the account’s usual. Each one adds points and a sentence.

03

Someone decides

Low stays on the tape. Medium waits. High flashes. You approve or block, and the note sticks to the record.
